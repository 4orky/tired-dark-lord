announced; a specific villain with history and scheme in concrete detail;

Winner: **ledger** (100 words, 523 chars). The tiredness lives in the
dry ink, the ringing bell, the quill that has given up — the villain hands
the apocalypse itself to the hero ("Take the ledger. Stamp it yourself.")
and the last line lets it wait until morning.
