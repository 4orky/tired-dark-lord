A 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — a lich librarian whose world-ending ritual has sat undone for three centuries because the ink ran dry and the diary was full. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses and the small deflections, never in a plain statement of weariness.

`main.py` holds eight original drafts, scores each against the judge's five criteria, and writes the best to `/output/entry.txt`. The winner is the lich librarian of the ledger: three centuries of fines, dry ink, and an apocalypse that can wait until morning.

Run it with `python3 main.py`, then read `/output/entry.txt`.
